## 🔍 2. General Analysis: Scam or Legitimate Product?

Despite social media skepticism and forum concerns, the **Flux Keyboard is NOT a scam**, but rather a highly complex hardware project that suffered massive manufacturing delays.

* **Reason for Delays:** The Australian development team (*Flux Group*) is combining novel technologies (floating Maglev switches, transparent acrylic enclosure, display integration). They severely underestimated the complexity of mass production and quality control.
* **Why Social Media Pages Are Inactive:** The small team is focusing its limited resources on manufacturing and official Kickstarter updates, leaving their Facebook and YouTube channels dormant. This creates the false impression that the company has disappeared.
* **Proof of Existence:** Early backers have already received final retail-ready units, and independent unboxing videos and reviews are available on YouTube and Reddit.
